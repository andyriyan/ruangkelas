1. Segitiga Siku-siku

Trigonometri dipelajari pertama kali pada segitiga siku-siku, yaitu segitiga yang memiliki satu sudut \(90°\).

Komponen segitiga siku-siku (relatif terhadap sudut \(\theta\)):
• Sisi depan (opposite) — sisi di seberang sudut \(\theta\)
• Sisi samping (adjacent) — sisi yang berdekatan dengan \(\theta\) (bukan hipotenusa)
• Hipotenusa (hypotenuse) — sisi terpanjang, di seberang sudut siku-siku
Teorema Pythagoras (pengingat): \[c^2 = a^2 + b^2\] di mana \(c\) = hipotenusa, \(a\) dan \(b\) = sisi tegak lainnya.

2. Rasio Trigonometri

Enam rasio trigonometri utama untuk sudut \(\theta\) dalam segitiga siku-siku:

SOH-CAH-TOA: \[ \sin\theta = \frac{\text{depan}}{\text{hipotenusa}} \qquad \cos\theta = \frac{\text{samping}}{\text{hipotenusa}} \qquad \tan\theta = \frac{\text{depan}}{\text{samping}} \]
FungsiDefinisiKebalikan (Resiprokal)
\(\sin\theta\)\(\dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}}\)\(\csc\theta = \dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\)
\(\cos\theta\)\(\dfrac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}\)\(\sec\theta = \dfrac{1}{\cos\theta}\)
\(\tan\theta\)\(\dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}\)\(\cot\theta = \dfrac{1}{\tan\theta}\)
💡 Mnemonik: SOH–CAH–TOA → Sine = Opposite/Hypotenuse, Cosine = Adjacent/Hypotenuse, Tangent = Opposite/Adjacent

3. Nilai Sudut Istimewa

Hafal tabel berikut untuk mempercepat perhitungan:

Sudut (\(\theta\)) \(0°\)\(30°\)\(45°\)\(60°\)\(90°\)
\(\sin\theta\) \(0\) \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) \(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\) \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\) \(1\)
\(\cos\theta\) \(1\) \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\) \(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\) \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) \(0\)
\(\tan\theta\) \(0\) \(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\) \(1\) \(\sqrt{3}\) tak tentu
💡 Trik hafal \(\sin\): \(\sin 0° = \tfrac{\sqrt{0}}{2}\), \(\sin 30° = \tfrac{\sqrt{1}}{2}\), \(\sin 45° = \tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\), \(\sin 60° = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\), \(\sin 90° = \tfrac{\sqrt{4}}{2}\)

4. Identitas Trigonometri Dasar

Identitas Pythagoras: \[\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1\] \[1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta\] \[1 + \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta\]
Hubungan tan dan cot: \[\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} \qquad \cot\theta = \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\]

5. Tanda pada Setiap Kuadran

Pada koordinat kartesius, tanda nilai trigonometri bergantung pada kuadran sudut:

KuadranSudut\(\sin\)\(\cos\)\(\tan\)
I\(0° - 90°\)+++
II\(90° - 180°\)+−−
III\(180° - 270°\)−−+
IV\(270° - 360°\)−+−
💡 Mnemonik: "Semua Siswa Tekun Cerdas" → Kuadran I,II,III,IV: semua +, Sin +, Tan +, Cos + (berlawanan arah jarum jam mulai kuadran I).

6. Contoh Soal

📝 Soal 1 — Diketahui segitiga siku-siku dengan sisi depan = 3 dan hipotenusa = 5. Tentukan \(\sin\theta\), \(\cos\theta\), dan \(\tan\theta\).
  1. 1 Sisi samping = \(\sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = \sqrt{16} = 4\).
  2. 2 \(\sin\theta = \dfrac{3}{5}\), \(\cos\theta = \dfrac{4}{5}\), \(\tan\theta = \dfrac{3}{4}\). ✅
📝 Soal 2 — Hitung nilai \(\sin 135°\).
  1. 1 \(135° = 180° - 45°\) → berada di Kuadran II (sin positif).
  2. 2 \(\sin(180° - 45°) = \sin 45° = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\). ✅
📝 Soal 3 — Sederhanakan \(\dfrac{\sin^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta} + 1\).
\[\frac{\sin^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta} + 1 = \tan^2\theta + 1 = \sec^2\theta\]

✅ Menggunakan identitas Pythagoras \(1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta\).

⚠️ Perhatian: \(\cos 90° = 0\), sehingga \(\tan 90°\) dan \(\sec 90°\) tidak terdefinisi!
